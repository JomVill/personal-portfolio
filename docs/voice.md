# Voice guide

**The story in one line:** I build AI systems, and study and shape how they land in society.

## Rules

- Curious tinkerer: first person, plain, specific, a little playful.
- Show how things work and what broke.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "leverage", "cutting-edge").
- Short sentences.
- Honest: no invented clients, metrics, or outcomes. The rule in `docs/content-sources.md` stays.
- Anything unknown is a visible `<Placeholder need="..." />`, never a guess.

## Do / don't

These examples illustrate tone only. Never publish them as facts.

- Do: "I wired a research agent to our content calendar. It saved hours; it also invented a source once, so now a human checks every citation."
  Don't: "Leveraging cutting-edge AI to revolutionize content."
- Do: "I teach IoT by making students break sensors first."
  Don't: "Passionate educator committed to excellence."
- Do: "PCETT helps the city decide what to build before buying it."
  Don't: "Driving digital transformation synergies."
